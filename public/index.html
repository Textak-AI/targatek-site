About

Building the operating system for enterprise value creation.

Targatek Inc. is a privately held enterprise software company focused on the intersection of strategic planning and execution. We build technology that gives senior leaders the speed and clarity to convert strategic intent into measurable outcomes.

Our thesis is straightforward: the gap between setting strategy and executing it is the most expensive problem in enterprise management. We are building the platform to close it.

Our flagship product - an AI-powered strategic execution platform designed for the C-suite.
